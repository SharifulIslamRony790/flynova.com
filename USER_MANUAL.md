## 1. For Customers

### Account Registration & Login
To use FlyNova's booking services, you need to create an account.
1. **Sign Up**: Click on the "Sign Up" button on the top right. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, and a secure password.
2. **Login**: If you already have an account, click "Login" and enter your credentials. You can also sign in directly using your **Google Account**.

### Searching for Services
You can search for services directly from the Home Page:
- **Flights**: Enter your Origin, Destination, Date, number of Passengers, and optionally filter by Price Range.
- **Hotels**: Enter a City or Hotel Name and select your preferred Price Range.
- **Holiday Packages**: Enter a destination and choose a price tier to see available tour packages.

### Booking & Payment Process
1. **Select a Service**: Once you find a suitable flight, hotel, or package, click on **Book Now** or **View Details**.
2. **Review Details**: Review the summary and the total price.
3. **Checkout**: Click **Proceed to Checkout**.
4. **Payment**: 
   - FlyNova supports secure payments via **SSLCommerz**.
   - Select your preferred payment method (VISA, MasterCard, bKash, Nagad).
   - Enter your mobile number or card details.
   - Once the payment is successful, your booking status will automatically change to **Confirmed**.

### Managing Your Bookings
- **My Bookings Dashboard**: Go to the dropdown menu under your profile picture and click on **My Bookings**.
- Here, you can see all your past and upcoming trips.
- **Download/Print Tickets**: For confirmed bookings, click the **Print Ticket** or **Download PDF** button to save a copy of your itinerary.

## 2. For Administrators

### Accessing the Admin Dashboard
1. Go to the URL: `yourdomain.com/dashboard/` (or click "Admin Dashboard" in your profile menu if you are a staff member).
2. The Dashboard Home will show you real-time metrics: Total Revenue, Active Bookings, Pending Bookings, and Total Customers.

### Managing Services
From the left sidebar, you can easily add, edit, or delete inventory:
- **Flights**: Add new flights, assign airlines, and set departure/arrival times and pricing.
- **Hotels**: Manage hotel details, star ratings, and upload images.
- **Packages**: Create new holiday packages, upload banners, and set the duration (days/nights).

### Viewing & Updating Bookings
- Go to the **Bookings** tab to see all customer reservations.
- If a payment was made offline or needs manual approval, you can edit the booking and change the status from **Pending** to **Confirmed**.

### Managing Users
- Go to the **Customers** tab to view all registered users.
- Click on a customer's name to view their profile, past bookings, and total amount spent on the platform.
- You can suspend or block a user directly from their profile page if necessary.
